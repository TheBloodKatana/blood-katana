Blood Katana · Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Blood Katana is a single-player game made by Weird Animations. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

We do not run any servers and we do not collect, sell or store personal information ourselves. There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one (for example to revive once in a run, get an extra daily spin, or double the shards you earned). These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice. Google's use of this information is described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (cosmetic items and koban, the in-game currency) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase happened so it can unlock the item, and can ask Google Play to restore items you already own.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your progress (shards, koban, unlocks, cosmetics, feats, records) and your saved run are stored only on your own device, inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If your phone's backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
