Your blade was broken. Your name was stolen. Between you and the man who did it stand a hundred floors of hell.

Blood Katana is a fast samurai roguelite where every cut is drawn by your own hand. Tap to slash, swipe to cut, hold to draw, and trace shapes in the air to unleash techniques while time slows around you.

• Ink chains: hold still mid-stroke and draw again to fuse two techniques into one.
• Parry by cutting through enemy strikes. Foes guard, dodge, feint, surround you and read your stance.
• Eleven weapons: pry the katana, yari, kanabō, kusarigama and teppō from the dead, or forge the nodachi, war fan, bō, kama and naginata at the shrine. Every one has its own moves, blood form and techniques.

• Feats, a bestiary, and eight wanderers who come to live at your shrine as you prove yourself.
• The Blood Tree: bank shards between runs and grow new techniques forever.
• Dress the part: outfits, blade finishes and slash colours. A free spin every day.
• Plays offline. Ads only when you choose to watch one. Optional purchases are cosmetic.

## Content rating notes (for the IARC questionnaire)
- Violence: fantasy violence against humans and creatures, with blood (pixel art; enemies can be cut in two).
- Simulated gambling: **yes**. A tanuki NPC plays chinchirorin (dice) for in-game coins or shards only, never for paid currency.
- In-app purchases: **yes** (cosmetic items and koban). Answer yes to "digital purchases".
- Random items: the daily spin. It cannot be bought; spins come free daily or from optional ads. Odds are shown on the spin screen.
- No sexual content, no strong language, no user-to-user interaction, no location sharing.
- Expect roughly PEGI 12 / ESRB Teen. Answer the questionnaire honestly; the rating is assigned from your answers.

## Data safety form (with AdMob)
Follow Google's own AdMob guidance for the Data safety form (search "AdMob data disclosure"). In short, declare that the app **collects and shares** for **advertising** (and analytics/fraud prevention, as the SDK does):
- Device or other IDs (advertising ID)
- Approximate location (from IP)
- App activity: app interactions; app info and performance: diagnostics
Data is encrypted in transit. Users can't request deletion from you (you hold none), but can reset their advertising ID. Purchases are handled by Google Play; the app itself stores nothing off the device.
