How to play
  • Every run starts at the Shrine of the Broken Blade. Spend banked shards on a bigger pouch or a deeper soul, commission a starting weapon, test techniques on the straw dummy, then walk through the gate.
  • Ten realms of ten floors each. Floor 1 is a store; on combat floors clear every room to unseal the stairs; floor 5 holds a mini boss and, past it, a rest place with a hot spring, an altar and a better merchant; floor 10 holds the realm's lord. Which lord and which warden you meet is random.
  • You carry four treasures and four boons, no doubles. A full pouch means choosing what to give up. The altar at the rest place trades one boon or reforges one treasure. Treasure rooms make you pick a treasure or a weapon.
  • Foes drop mon (spend it this run) and rarely blood shards. Shards are only banked at a realm's store floor or the rest place; die before that and they scatter.
  • The Blind Smith turns banked shards into the Blood Tree. Every branch ends in a technique (a circle, a V, a zigzag…) you trace with right-click. Hold still mid-trace to chain a second shape: both techniques fire, fused, for both recharges.
  • Lords paint glyphs in the air and are warded while they do. Trace the same shape before it's finished and their own brush cuts them. Miss it and the glyph lands on you.
  • Trails block cuts. Purple spirit cuts pass through trails; pink lunges are grabs that no parry or counter stance stops. Shields only face one way: flank them, hook them, or bring the kanabō. Ink monks wipe your trails and blot your brush.
  • Foes take turns, surround you, read your counter stances and punish whiffs, and they get sharper every realm. Hit a foe three times in a second and it breaks free; bosses deflect while they do.
  • Orange rooms hold a duelist. Optional, dangerous, well paid. Purple rooms hold strangers, and some of them want to play.
